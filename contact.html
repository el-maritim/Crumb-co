A few common questions

At the counter.

Can I reserve a loaf?

Absolutely. Give us a call or use the pickup form, ideally by the day before. We set aside a small number of loaves for regulars.

Do you have gluten-free bread?

Our kitchen works with wheat flour all day, so we can't promise a gluten-free environment. Please speak with us before ordering if you have an allergy.

What happens to the bread at the end of the day?

We share extra loaves with a nearby community fridge. Good bread should find a good home.
