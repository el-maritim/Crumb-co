# Crumb & Co. bakery site

An original, responsive multi-page bakery site inspired by the broad artisanal
bakery category. It does not reproduce Fournil's branding, writing, or layout.

## Preview

Open `index.html` in a browser. The pages are static and need no build step.

## Pages

- Home: `index.html`
- Menu: `menu.html` (interactive allergen filters)
- Story: `story.html`
- Field notes: `journal.html` and the three pages in `notes/`
- Visit and pickup request: `contact.html`

Navigation, typography, colors, responsive behavior, and footer styling are
shared across pages. The home page includes a pinned, scroll-linked bake
timeline on desktop and a touch-scrollable card rail on smaller screens. Motion
respects the user's reduced-motion preference.

## Before publishing

The address, opening hours, email, telephone number, menu, prices, and business
history are sample content. Replace them with verified details. The pickup and
newsletter interactions are front-end demos; they do not send data anywhere.
