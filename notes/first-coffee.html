Neighbourhood · 5 minute read

The first coffee is on us.

Before the lunch rush and after the oven goes on, the morning belongs to familiar faces and the first warm cup.

Every day, a few people arrive before the sun reaches the front window. There is the dog walker who orders an oat latte and a plain roll, the teacher with a thermos, and the neighbour who picks up three loaves for the block.

A counter is a little crossroads

We started keeping a pot ready for our earliest regulars. It was never a promotion; it just felt like the right thing to do on a cold morning. Now the coffee is part of our opening routine, just like checking the starter and laying out the first tray.

In a neighbourhood, the small rituals add up. A quick hello turns into a conversation. A loaf becomes a thank-you. Someone notices when you have been away for a week. The bakery is our workplace, but it belongs to all of us who pass through.

We saved you a spot

If you are new to the block, come introduce yourself. We will tell you what just came out of the oven, and you can tell us what you like on your toast.
